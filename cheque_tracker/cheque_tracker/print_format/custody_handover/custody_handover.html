{#- محضر تسليم شيك — Cheque Custody Handover (Cheque). A5. Clone of EEI's approved Delivery Note format (EEI_PRINT_DESIGN_REFERENCE §4): same tokens and table-based layout, its acknowledgment line, and its signature block pattern reduced from three boxes to two per §2 of the reference. Recipient resolution, in order: `current_holder` (a Link to User — the live internal custodian), then `external_holder` / the endorsement counterparty. The Cheque Event trail is deliberately NOT consulted for the recipient: `_append_event` stamps `to_holder` from `current_holder` as it stands when the row is written, so on an external hand-over the event still carries the employee who was about to be released. Reading it would name the wrong person on a custody record. The trail IS used for the giver, where `from_holder` is written explicitly and is the only record of who let the cheque go. Cairo is bundled with the app (public/fonts) and loaded from a site-relative @font-face, so the approved typeface survives PDF rendering. -#}
